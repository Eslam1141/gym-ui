## Muscle tags

Chest Dips: lower chest + triceps lateral. Hip Abduction Machine and Barbell Hip Thrust: glutes. Barbell Shrug: lower back / traps (the app has no separate traps region). Preacher Curl: biceps short head. Reverse Pec Deck: rear delt. Cable Lateral Raise: side delt. Weighted Cable Crunch: upper abs. Wide-grip Lat Pulldown: lats.

Side effect: the existing alias "crunch / sit up" (-> Weighted Cable Crunch) now resolves to a clip and muscle tag for AI-coach plans.
