Important Notes
Weekly schedule:
Sat Legs · Sun Push · Mon Pull · Tue rest (football) · Wed Lower · Thu Upper · Fri rest. Every muscle 2×/week with 3-4 days between.
Progressive overload:
add a little weight or a rep once you hit the top of the rep range with clean form — beat last session's numbers (shown on each card).
Train close to failure:
stop compound sets 1-3 reps short; isolation moves 0-1.
Warm-up:
follow the warm-up card before your first set.
Core 3×/week:
hanging leg raise (Pull), Pallof press (Upper), cable crunch (Lower). Brace as if bracing for a punch.
Recover:
7-9 h sleep. Football counts as leg load — keep it to Tuesday.
Deload
every 5-8 weeks: an easy week at ~⅔ the weight or half the sets.
Protein:
1.6-2.2 g per kg body weight daily.
