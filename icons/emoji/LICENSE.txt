frames and recompressed as animated WebP (<name>.webp). <name>-static.webp is
the first frame, used when the visitor prefers reduced motion.

Files: muscle (U+1F4AA), fire (U+1F525), sprout (U+1F331), rocket (U+1F680),
trophy (U+1F3C6), medal (U+1F947, first-place medal, as U+1F3C5 has no
animated version), crown (U+1F451), battery (U+1F50B).
