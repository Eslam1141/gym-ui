Warm-up — do this first

  1. Raise your temperature — 5 min easy cardio (bike, incline walk or rower) until you break a light sweat.
  2. Loosen the day's joints — 5-8 slow reps each. Upper-body days: band pull-aparts, shoulder dislocates, arm circles, cat-cow. Lower-body days: leg swings front & side, hip circles, 10 bodyweight squats, walking lunges.
  3. Ramp up the first lift only — empty bar ×10, then ~50% ×5, ~70% ×3, ~85% ×1. Rest 60-90 s and stop each set well short of effort.
  4. Start working set 1. Isolation moves (raises, curls, extensions) need just one light feeder set.
